If I joined PURE Energy, I would build a dealer workspace that turns lead hand-offs into measurable customer outcomes. First, I would connect the router to dealer response times, stock availability, installation capacity, and warranty coverage so assignments reflect who can serve a customer today, not only who owns a pincode. Next, I would give dealers a simple mobile-first queue with one-tap follow-up, visit scheduling, quotation tracking, and clear reasons for lost leads. Shared status definitions and lightweight coaching would help managers spot delays without creating extra paperwork. Finally, I would measure outcomes by source, product, territory, and dealer while limiting personal data to authorized users. A phased pilot in Sangareddy would establish reliable baselines, collect dealer feedback, and test whether better routing improves contact speed and conversion before expanding across the network. Dealer feedback would shape each phase, keep workflows grounded, and make service improvements visible across the network.
